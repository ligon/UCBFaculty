* Employment Nondiscrimination and Academic Freedom Addendum (California v1.3)

** 1  Scope
This Addendum applies to any use of the Licensed Work in connection with
evaluating, hiring, appointing, compensating, promoting, or otherwise making
decisions about academic personnel. This includes, but is not limited to:

- Faculty and other academic employee appointments;
- Merit and promotion reviews; and
- *Appointments to administrative committees and committees of the Academic Senate,*
  which are recognized components of academic service and may bear on merit review and other personnel evaluations.

** 2  Federal and State Compliance
Users must employ the Licensed Work in full compliance with:

- Title VII of the U.S. Civil Rights Act of 1964 (42 U.S.C. §§2000e et seq.);
- California Fair Employment and Housing Act (Gov’t Code §12940 et seq.); and
- All other applicable federal, state, and local laws prohibiting employment discrimination.

These laws forbid discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex (including pregnancy,
gender identity, and sexual orientation), national origin, ancestry, age,
disability, medical condition, genetic information, marital status, and other protected characteristics.

** 3  Data and Design Restrictions
Developers and maintainers of derivative works must not introduce or enable the use of data,
variables, or criteria that directly identify or classify individuals by characteristics protected under Section 2,
*except where such information is demonstrably relevant and lawfully permissible as a bona fide occupational qualification* under applicable nondiscrimination law.

For users and developers subject to California law, this restriction must be interpreted in accordance with:

- California Proposition 209 (Cal. Const. Art. I § 31); and
- The limited bona fide occupational qualification exception recognized by Title VII and the California FEHA.

Permissible examples include only cases in which membership in a protected category is intrinsic to the legitimate duties of a specific appointment (e.g., service on a Committee on the Status of Women).  
Any broader use of such characteristics in the design, implementation, or operation of the Work or its derivatives is prohibited.

** 4  Political or Ideological Neutrality
Use of the Licensed Work must conform to:

- California Labor Code §§1101–1102 (prohibiting discrimination or interference with lawful political activity); and
- UC APM-010 (Academic Freedom), APM-015 (Faculty Code of Conduct), and APM-035 (Affirmative Action and Nondiscrimination in Employment).

Users must not employ the Work to advantage or disadvantage individuals because of their lawful political beliefs, affiliations, or expression.

** 5  Academic Freedom
Decisions informed by the Licensed Work must respect the principles of academic freedom as articulated in the AAUP 1940 Statement and UC APM-010.

** 6  Certification
By using this Work for any academic employment, appointment, or service-related purpose, you certify compliance with these provisions.

** 7  Termination
Any violation of this Addendum automatically terminates the license granted herein.

** 9  Interpretation and Updating of References
References in this Addendum to statutes, regulations, or University of California policies shall be understood to include those provisions *as they may be amended, renumbered, or succeeded from time to time.*  
If any referenced authority is repealed or replaced, users shall interpret this Addendum to preserve its original intent: to ensure nondiscriminatory, lawful, and academically sound use of the Licensed Work in connection with academic employment or service decisions.
